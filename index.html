What makes it custom
Custom Reserve

Then the part that makes it yours.

01Custom escalation logic

Not a generic urgency score. Rules built around what actually constitutes an emergency in your business — a filing deadline, a payer denial, a key client going quiet for eleven days, a specific name in a subject line.

02Custom assignment and routing

Who handles what, encoded. Which paralegal owns which matter type, which biller handles which payer, what goes to the office manager, what waits for the weekly review. Most things shouldn't reach you at all.

03Custom process flows

Multi-step workflows that mirror how your business actually moves work, start to finish. Intake through conflict check through engagement letter. Referral through insurance verification through pre-visit packet.

04Custom analytics and reporting

Built around the numbers you actually manage by — response time by client tier, matter activity aging, referral source performance, payer correspondence aging — delivered in the format and on the schedule you'll actually read.

If your data can't leave

The Regulated Practice Layer.

For practices with real compliance obligations, we offer this as a paid add-on. Depending on your situation you take one path or the other — they are two different configurations, and they aren't combinable.

Path one

Zero data retention

Prompts and responses are never stored. The usual choice for legal, finance and HR work.

Path two

HIPAA-ready

A signed Business Associate Agreement, with documented retention rules. The path for practices handling PHI.

$250 / month on Reserve · $400 / month on Custom Reserve
Why we charge for it. Zero retention at the model layer means nothing if the meeting recorder, CRM and mail vendor all retain data. An honest end-to-end claim means auditing every vendor in the chain — which is work, and which is why it isn't bundled free.
What's included
  • Zero data retention or HIPAA-ready configuration
  • DPA or BAA as applicable
  • A restricted, vetted tool stack — every vendor in the chain checked for how they handle retention
  • Documented retention and deletion rules by data type
  • Audit logging
  • A written data flow map

The document worth having on its own

A written map showing exactly where your information travels and what is kept at each step. Most practices need one for their own compliance posture and can't produce it.
